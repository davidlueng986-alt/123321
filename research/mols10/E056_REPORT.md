**Mathematical proof of optimization completeness:** For each of six classes, let f_k(c) be the number of dual coefficients equal to offset c. We maximize sum f_k(c_k) subject to sum c_k=0. If two offsets are absent from their class support, changing one to any supported coefficient and changing the other to preserve zero sum cannot decrease the score, and strictly increases it. Therefore an optimum exists with at most one unsupported offset. Enumerate which class is balancing; equivalently use branch-and-bound over five supported offsets, with the sixth determined by zero sum. The implementation chooses a fixed class order and explores five supported values; this is complete by the same argument, and optimistic suffix maxima prune only branches that cannot beat the incumbent.
