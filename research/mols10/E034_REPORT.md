## Exact model and symmetry

Let A,B,C be order-n Latin squares. Normalize each first row to identity by its own symbol permutation, and reorder rows by A's first-column symbols. Without loss of generality, A[r,0]=r. After swapping B and C if needed, B[1,0]<C[1,0]. These are sound existence-preserving symmetries. By E033's proved simultaneous column/symbol conjugacy, one A second-row derangement `f` of each of the 12 cycle types (n=10) covers all possible triples *provided all branches for each f are completely handled*. Our actual n=10 global runs are **not complete**.

Source: `E034_direct_solver.cpp`. It tracks row/column Latin uniqueness and all three pair overlays exactly, branches on the most constrained *cell* and its allowed (a,b,c) triple, and can use necessary filters `T` (residual triangle support), `U` (unit propagation over uncovered pair edges), `I` (bounded integral triangle exact cover, incomplete searches retain the branch), `C` (column-aware candidate triangles), and `H` (row Hall necessary condition). The solver distinguishes SAT / exhaustive normalized UNSAT / UNKNOWN_BUDGET. Positive candidate matrices are independently replayed in Python. Any full positive witness would be written to `E034_FULL_TRIPLE_WITNESS.json` only following independent exact 100/100/100 replay; **that file does not exist in this run**.

**New necessary condition — column-aware residual triangles.** Given k completed rows and an unused symbol pair (A=a,B=b), any future cell realizing it must have some symbol c such that (a,c) is unused in AC, (b,c) is unused in BC, and some *single future column j* has symbols a,b,c all still unused in that column in A,B,C respectively. Remove a candidate (a,b,c) triangle if no such column exists. Every complete solution necessarily meets this test. Unit propagation and integral exact-cover on this smaller residual triangle hypergraph are also sound necessary obstructions, but the integral solver is **relaxed**: it does not enforce that all remaining chosen triples fit distinct row/column cells. A `SAT_RESIDUAL_ONLY` is not a triple.

## Actual experiments, strict results

1. Independent small-order regression controls: order 2 and 3 exhaustive UNSAT; order 4 and 5 SAT with independent Python Latin / all-three overlays n² verification; order 6 exhaustive normalized UNSAT with ~13.6 million C++ nodes under `TIUC`. A first C++ prototype mistakenly reset the current row's state when recursively visiting deeper rows, and initially failed the n=5 positive control. That failure was discarded, the per-depth state corrected, and the full controls rerun. A later prefix-only test briefly reported a spurious successful *six-row snapshot* because the goal check preceded the necessary filters and the captured best-prefix snapshot was stale. Both issues were corrected; its first output was discarded, and the corrected prefix independently checked. None affected the final matrix validation.
2. Global n=10 sweep: each of the 12 E033 canonical f cycle types was tried with a distinct deterministic seed, 20,000,000 node budget/type, `TIUC` column-aware filters. **12/12 `UNKNOWN_BUDGET`**, no full witness and **zero complete cycle-type exclusions**. Further 25-second searches for the (5+5), (10), (3+7), and (2+2+2+2+2) types all returned `UNKNOWN_BUDGET` and failed to find a six-row prefix surviving the strong residual filter in their sampled branch order. This is not exhaustive coverage. A previous long multiseed run timed out externally; only completed cases are counted.
3. Egan–Wanless near-triple: independent replay of the displayed A/B/C gives overlays **100/100/91**. Of all C(10,4)=210 four-row subsets, exactly **39** retain no repeat BC pair (AB,AC are automatically unique); exactly 9 valid five-row subsets; zero valid six-row subsets. Any complete triple agreeing with all three displayed near-triple squares on 4 intact rows must contain one of these 39 four-row prefixes up to harmless relabeling and row reordering.
4. Construction attempt: normalized the 39 complete four-row prefixes and allowed all remaining six rows to vary with exact Latin/orthogonality constraints. All **39/39** instances were returned **`UNSAT_EXHAUSTIVE_NORMALIZED`** by the final C++ solver under finite budgets that were not reached; combined 497,846,358 DFS nodes (not a measure of global coverage). The solver's remaining-row A first-column ordering is safe because the free rows can be reordered. Six immediate root UNSATs were independently reproduced by a separate Python column-aware unit-propagation checker. One representative (`[0,1,2,3]`) was independently rerun *without the U/I pruning mechanisms*: `TC` returned exhaustive UNSAT after 11,347,654 nodes. A weaker `T`-only version timed out and was correctly classified UNKNOWN. These checks reduce but do not eliminate code/certification risks; no separate general LRAT certificate exists.
5. A crucial construction failure: one six-row partial triple passed the **column-free** residual 40-triangle exact cover, but 20 of those symbol triples had no available future column. Thus the residual exact-cover SAT was a false indicator of Latin extendability, not a full triple. This motivated the stronger C filter. All reported prefix-only candidates have been independently checked; no complete candidate was found.

**Restricted interpretation:** In the displayed near-triple's fixed labels, an exact full triple cannot be obtained by changing cells in at most six *entire rows* (keeping the other four rows completely unchanged in all three squares), **conditional on correctness of the complete C++ enumerations**. This is a local computational exclusion, NOT a theorem ruling out all order-10 triples. In particular, changing columns and symbols under different labelings is not covered by the stated row-Hamming restriction.
