## Exact construction and independent replay

- The 10 blocks of a 2-(6,3,2) design are encoded in `generate_calderbank_fishburn.py`; complement blocks comprise the other 10 subsets of size 3 of 6 indices.
- `R`: the 32 six-vectors with all coordinates 1 or 2 and an even number of 2s.
- `D`, `Dbar`: for each chosen 3-support, all eight assignments of nonzero coordinates, totalling 80 points for each of the two disjoint support families.
- `U`: 12 vectors of Hamming weight 1 in F_3^6.
- Dimension 7 slices: `(0,R∪D)` of size 112; `(1,R∪Dbar)` of size 112; `(2,U)` of size 12.

**Total** 112+112+12 = **236** distinct vectors. `verify_capset.py` checks all 27,730 unordered pairs using exact mod-3 arithmetic and accepts the 236-line static witness in `candidates/calderbank_fishburn_236.txt`.

## Saturation result and a rigorous local-reconstruction obstruction

For each `p ∉ A`, let `s(p)` be the number of unordered pairs `{x,y} ⊂ A` such that `x+y+p=0` mod 3. For a fixed `p`, these secants have pairwise disjoint endpoints in `A`: each `x` has exactly one possible partner `y=-p-x`. Thus to include `p` in a modified cap `(A∖R)∪B`, the removed set `R` must contain one endpoint from EACH of the `s(p)` disjoint pairs, and necessarily `|R|≥s(p)`.

Exhaustive exact calculation:
- `|F_3^7∖A| = 1,951`;
- all 1,951 are blocked (the 236-cap is complete);
- `min s(p) = 10`, `max s(p) = 56`;
- incidence histogram `(s : number of outside points)`:
  `10:24, 11:416, 12:480, 13:160, 14:120, 15:192, 16:224, 18:240, 25:60, 30:32, 38:1, 56:2`;
- sum of secant incidences = `27,730 = 236×235/2`, an exact cross-check.

**Consequence for searching near this configuration:** no modified cap can contain any *new* point while keeping at least 227 of the 236 original points. To beat 236, any edit around this starting configuration must remove >=10 originals and add at least 11 outsiders. This is a mathematical constraint on edits around the particular published construction, NOT an upper bound on all caps of F_3^7, and the minimum of 10 is only a necessary condition.

## The cross-slice rigidity

Holding fixed both 112-point slices `A0,A1`, every point in the third slice except the 12 vectors `(2,U)` is forbidden by a line connecting an `A0` point to an `A1` point:
- 717 of 729 points in slice 2 are blocked by cross-slice secants;
- exactly the 12 existing `U` points are compatible;
- each of these 717 excluded points has >=11 cross-slice secants with disjoint endpoints.

Thus with `A0,A1` frozen, the 12-point third slice is uniquely determined; introducing even one other third-slice point demands removing **at least 11** points from the first two slices. This too is a property of this specific construction.
