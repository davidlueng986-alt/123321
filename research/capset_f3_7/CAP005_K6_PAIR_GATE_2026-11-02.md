# CAP-005E — Exact six-fiber pair-removal gate (2026-11-02)

## Statement and proof
Continue from CAP-005D (CI 41369606221), which counted individually admissible outsider points for each of the 296,010 six-fiber release neighborhoods of the independently checked classical 236-point cap B in F3^7.

CAP-002A independently established by exhaustive exact enumeration that any two distinct points outside B, if both added to a cap, require deleting **at least 16** points of B. This follows from minimum vertex cover of the union of their original secant matchings, plus forced deletion of the third point on the line through the two outsiders if it belongs to B. That union is a disjoint union of paths and even cycles, and the vertex-cover calculation is exact. We independently reimplemented this enumeration in C++17, using a separate parsing/encoding/DSU implementation, confirming **1,902,225** outsider pairs, minimum **16** removals, with **888** pairs attaining the minimum. The new C++ verifier also directly checks the fixture contains no three-term progression.

For a six-fiber release set T, let N(T) be the number of outsiders passing CAP-005D's *necessary* secant-cover gate. Any cap that retains all B points outside T and improves upon |B|=236 must insert at least 17 outsiders: inserting one forces >=10 deletions; inserting two or more forces >=16 deletions, so <=16 insertions cannot improve. Therefore **N(T) <=16 rigorously rules out improvement**, without needing a MILP/UNSAT certificate. Conversely N(T)>=17 is only a necessary condition, not a witness.
